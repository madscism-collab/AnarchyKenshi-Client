KENSHI MULTIPLAYER (AnarchyKenshi) - PRIVACY NOTICE
Version 1.2, 19 November 2026

WHO
Anarchy, the author of the Mod. Contact: our Discord,
https://discord.gg/33TGnXAxBQ

WHAT WE PROCESS
When you play online: your account name and login data (your password
itself never leaves your PC), your characters and what you do in the game,
chat, your IP address, and technical information about your device and your
game.
The launcher checks for updates online, and the server browser contacts the
listed servers; like any website, they see your IP address.

WHY
To run the online game, keep your characters and fix bugs, and - in our
legitimate interest and that of all players - to keep the game free of
cheating and abuse. Nothing is sold or used for advertising.

SERVERS
Servers may be located in any country. Servers run by other people follow
their own rules, not this notice.

HOW LONG
Accounts and characters: until you ask us to delete them or the world is
wiped. Logs: only as long as needed for these purposes. Ban data: as long
as the ban lasts.

YOUR RIGHTS
You can ask what we keep about you, ask us to correct it or delete your
account, or object to how we use it - write to us on Discord. If you live in
the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.
Deleting an account does not lift a ban.

DISCORD
Our Discord server follows Discord's own privacy policy. Our bot reads
messages in our channels to fight spam and scams. In some channels it
replies with the help of an AI service, which then receives that channel's
recent messages.

Don't share anything sensitive in chat.

CHANGES
We may update this notice. The current version is always at
https://github.com/madscism-collab/AnarchyKenshi-Client/blob/main/PRIVACY.txt
